Be a Pimp - Prostitute Manager  (CET mod, version 1.1.0)
Credits and thanks
===========================================================================

OpenSex Manager
  The scene engine (workspots through AMM), the position sliders, the undress-by-appearance-tags method, the look
  restore check and the Bag of Dicks equip method were adapted from "OpenSex Manager" (same author as this mod).
  Core/cron.lua (timers) comes from "Dynamic KillCAM" (same author as this mod).

Pose data used for labels and categories (Data/pose_meta.lua is generated from it):
  OpenSex (CC BY-SA 4.0) - animations by acepleiades (313 scenes) and Frolique (4 scenes); scene data by acepleiades,
    VersuchDrei, DragoFall.  https://github.com/acepleiades/OpenSex
  OStimNG (GPLv3) - VersuchDrei et al., animations by Ace and Moon.  https://github.com/VersuchDrei/OStimNG
  Ported to Cyberpunk 2077 / AMM by blackpotoftea (NightCityNet).
  The original authors did not make this mod and do not endorse it.

Pleasures of Night City VOL.1 (OPTIONAL, not included)
  Extra animations by PinkyDude, installed in AMM (Collabs/Custom Poses). When present, its 28 two-person poses
  (missionary, doggy, cowgirl, oral) appear in the pose list with the prefix "PoNC". The author did not make this mod
  and does not endorse it.

Night City Allies
  The way NPCs are walked, held and driven (AI move / hold / mount / drive commands, navmesh points, traffic lanes)
  follows the approach of "Night City Allies". None of its files are included.

GTA JoyRide  (the author's own mod)
  The JoyRide mode (Core/joyride.lua) reuses the mechanics of the author's mod "GTA JoyRide": how it finds the people around the car
  (targeting system search), the seats (seat_back_left / seat_back_right), the rocking (the hops, the 15 s, the sound 1.5 s after the
  start), the rear free camera, the "drop me where it is busy" rule, the "drive to a quiet zone" rule (nobody around before the car rocks) and
the way the passenger is swapped for a controllable copy (DynamicEntitySpec) and mounted with AIMountCommand. The three rocking sounds (man + woman, man + man,
  woman + woman) come from it too: they are in r6/audioware/BeAGigolo as pimp_joyride_mf / _mm / _ff, so both mods can be installed
  together. No voice lines are used.

SECURITY  (the author's own mod)
  How guards and hired workers get and use weapons (Core/ent.lua, Core/guards.lua) follows the author's mod "SECURITY": real items from
  the NPC's inventory (GetItemList + the "Weapon" tag), AIEquipCommand, AISwitchToPrimary/SecondaryWeaponCommand, AIUnequipCommand,
  AINoRole for a guard standing at his post and AIFollowerRole (follows the player, friendly attitude) while he fights.
  The JoyRide client uses the same follower role and, like GTA JoyRide, a spawned copy of the passer-by that obeys commands.

Sex Framework  (the author's own mod)
  The way the window finds the car V aims at (Ent.VehicleLookedAt: the crosshair object the way AMM asks it, then the vehicle closest
  to the crosshair, then a car right next to V) and how a car record gets its name come from the author's mod "Sex Framework".
  The Client Sex Park rocks the car with the same hops and sounds as GTA JoyRide - without its rear camera.

Bag of Dicks (dp77_bagofdicks)  -  REQUIRED, not included
  Every man in a scene (clients and male workers) automatically gets the item "Items.dp77_bagofdicks_large"
  (or regular / extra large, by the setting). The mod is not included - install it from its own Nexus page.
  Its author did not make this mod and does not endorse it.

Audioware  -  sounds (the .mp3 files in r6/audioware/BeAGigolo are placeholders).

Requires (not included): Cyber Engine Tweaks, Codeware, Appearance Menu Mod (MaximiliumM), the OpenSex pose pack
(installed in AMM), Bag of Dicks, Audioware. Optional: Pleasures of Night City VOL.1.
